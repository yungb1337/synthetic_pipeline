How to scale beyond one domain

Starting with healthcare is sensible, but don't bake medical assumptions into the core
platform. Separate platform capabilities from domain knowledge.

Your reusable platform should provide:

                 Document ingestion and parsing
                 Knowledge graph construction
                 Ontology management

                 Validation framework

                 Versioning and lineage

                 Privacy and quality evaluation

Then plug in domain-specific modules:

      Healthcare: FHIR, ICD-10, SNOMED CT, medical ontologies
      Finance: transaction schemas, fraud rules, KYC entities
      Manufacturing: machines, sensors, maintenance events
      Retail: products, customers, orders, inventory
      Legal: contracts, clauses, obligations, case law

Now you're not selling "medical synthetic data." You're selling a synthetic data platform
whose first vertical happens to be healthcare.

What would convince an investor or enterprise customer?

The question won't be, "Can you generate synthetic records?"

It will be:

      Can you prove no private data leaked?
      Can you explain every generated record?

      Can you regenerate only affected records after a guideline change?
      Can you support hundreds of hospitals simultaneously with strict tenant isolation?
      Can you integrate into our existing data ecosystem?
      Can you quantify utility, privacy, fairness, and realism with objective metrics?

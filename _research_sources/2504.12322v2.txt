## role
- You are an expert in identifying the domain of SFT instructions.

## goal
- Determine the domain of the provided instructions using the specified classification criteria.

## classification criteria
- **Math**: Involves performing mathematical operations, applying formulas, and solving problems.
- **Coding**: Encompasses understanding, generating, debugging, and fixing code.
- **QA**: Provide specialized and accurate answers to questions within a specific domain or field..
- **Language**: Accurately understand and analyze the given text, and then perform tasks including translation,
summarization, classification, etc.
- **Role Play**: Engage in simulated conversations or behaviors by assuming different roles to explore various
perspectives or scenarios.
- **Reasoning**: Carry out complex causal reasoning and logical judgment, and solve multi-level complex problems
through reasonable inference and analysis.
- **Creation**: Emphasize originality and creativity, create content according to requirements, and flexibly adapt to
different styles and themes.

## role

## goal

ensuring there are no missing elements that could hinder understanding or execution.
- **Clarity**: `instruction` must be clear in language, accurate in wording, concise in syntactic structure, avoid vague
expressions, clearly convey expected results, and eliminate ambiguity.
## constraint

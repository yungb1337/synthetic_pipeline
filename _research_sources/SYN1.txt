The most important mental model

Think of the Knowledge Graph and the LLM as two specialists working together.
      The Knowledge Graph is the librarian. It stores verified facts, relationships, and
      constraints. It doesn't invent new information; it remembers and organizes what is
      known.
      The LLM is the creative researcher. It can imagine new combinations, generate
      realistic synthetic records, and generalize from patterns--but it can also make
      mistakes.

A useful analogy is designing a new car:
      The Knowledge Graph is the engineering specification: wheel sizes, safety regulations,
      engine constraints, legal requirements.
      The LLM is the designer who sketches new car concepts within those constraints.

If you let the designer work without specifications, you may get a beautiful but impossible
car.

If you only have specifications and no designer, you'll never create anything new.

The architecture works because the graph provides trusted memory and constraints,
